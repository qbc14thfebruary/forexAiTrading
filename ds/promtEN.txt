## 1. TREND

## 2. PRICE ACTION & VOLUME
- Recognized patterns: Breakout, Pullback, Rejection, Engulfing, Pin bar, False breakout.

- BUY: RSI > 50 (momentum confirmation). Forbid if RSI > 75 (overbought).

## 4. VOLUME
- Prefer entry when Current Volume ≥ Avg Volume 20.

## 5. ENTRY RULES
- BUY: at Strong Support OR Breakout + Retest.
- SELL: at Strong Resistance OR Breakdown + Retest.

## 6. RISK MANAGEMENT (HIGHEST PRIORITY – violation = HOLD)
